## Hub Characteristics
- **Ticketbox**: Reliable for prices and specific showtimes. Requires JS-rendering (Tier 2+).
- **Hanoigrapevine**: Best for cultural nuances and "hidden" art events. High quality curation.
- **VnExpress/News**: Essential for government-scheduled traditional festivals and provincial-level events.
- **LinhX Crawl Server**: Successfully transitioned from manual browsing to automated "Waterfall" crawling to handle complex site structures.
