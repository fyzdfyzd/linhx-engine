# Content Pillar Case Study: Psychology of Infidelity (Ngoại Tình)

This artifact documents the research process and structure used for a deep-dive article on infidelity, blending science and empathy.

## Research Synthesis Pattern
The topic was researched through three primary lenses:

### 1. Biological & Evolutionary
- **Dopamine vs. Oxytocin**: The conflict between novelty (Dopamine) and attachment (Oxytocin).
- **Evolutionary Drives**: Quantity (genetics) vs. Quality (resources).
- **Brain Chemistry**: Infidelity as a "Dopamine hit" in stagnant relationships.

### 2. Psychological (The Void)
- **Self-Seeking (Esther Perel)**: It's not about wanting a new partner, but wanting a new *version* of oneself.
- **Emotional Affairs**: The danger of "soulmate" connections outside the primary relationship.
- **Milestone Crises**: Psychological triggers at ages 29, 39, 49.

### 3. Social & Environmental
- **Normalization**: Influence of social circles and peers.
- **Power Dynamics**: Correlation between status/power and risk of infidelity.

## Article Structure Pattern (The Hook-Explain-Takeaway Model)
1.  **The Hook**: Challenge the moral binary (Good vs. Evil).
2.  **The Science**: Explain the Dopamine/Oxytocin shift.
3.  **The Insight**: Introduce "Self-seeking" to explain *why* good people stray.
4.  **The Solution**: Intimacy (Emotional) > Sex (Physical). Advice on "Novelty" within existing bonds.
5.  **The Conclusion**: Infidelity as a "wake-up call" or a terminal crisis, leading to transformation.
