# Personal Brand DTL: Overview

This Knowledge Item tracks the implementation details, content strategies, and research outcomes for the personal brand of **Đinh Trần Tuấn Linh (DTL)**.

## Core Strategy
The brand is positioned as a Thought Leader in **Technology, AI, and Humanity**. It focuses on:
- **Authenticity First**: Conversational, non-robotic voice.
- **Value-Driven**: Blending scientific facts with psychological depth.
- **Agentic Execution**: Using a multi-agent workforce (Brand Strategist, Researcher, Writer) to produce content.
