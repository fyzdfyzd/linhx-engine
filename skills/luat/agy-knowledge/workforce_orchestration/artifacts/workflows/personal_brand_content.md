# Personal Brand Content Workflow

This workflow ensures every post aligns with the personal brand identity while maximizing engagement through structured collaboration between strategist and manager roles.

## Workflow Overview

The process moves from broad strategic ideation to specific platform-native packaging to ensure consistency and reach.

## Phase 1: Ideation (Brand Strategist)
- **Trend Spotting**: Use web search to find relevant hot topics.
- **Brand Alignment**: Pass topics through the "Authenticity Filter" (unique perspective, relevance).
- **Outcome**: A entries in the content calendar with primary angles.

## Phase 2: Production (Writer/Strategist)
- **Core Drafting**: Write the primary long-form content or article.
- **Voice Check**: Ensure the tone matches the individual's established "voice samples."
- **Outcome**: A primary content artifact.

## Phase 3: Packaging (Social Media Manager)
- **Multi-Platform Adaptation**: Crafting specific variants for Facebook (casal/story), LinkedIn (professional/insight), and Threads (punchy).
- **Hook Polishing**: Iterating on the first 3 seconds to maximize scroll-stop.
- **Visual Briefing**: Defining the imagery or media needed to support the text.
- **Outcome**: Final, ready-to-post artifacts for each channel.

## Critical Rules
- **The "So What?" Test**: Every post must answer why the audience should care in the first sentence.
- **The "Authenticity" Test**: Content must sound like the individual, not a generic AI.
