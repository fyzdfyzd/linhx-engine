## Brief còn thiếu gì (checklist)

Ask about each item the brief does not answer:
- Budget range, number of locations, number of days, peak hours
- Brand guideline for mascot/logo; official assets (2D/3D/video); what the mascot may say or do; mascot voice
- Ambassador / celebrity usage rights
- KPI priority (awareness, UGC, data, sales) — decides the reward mechanic
- Existing systems: app, Zalo OA, CRM, POS voucher reconciliation; who owns the data
- Rewards: value, quantity, stock per day; promotion registration owner
- Venue: size, power, internet, noise, opening hours, mall rules
- Approval process and deadlines; who signs off scripts and visuals
- Reuse after the event (store, parties, schools)

## Idea scoring (1–5, 5 is best; for cost and ops 5 = cheap/easy)

| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Brand fit | Does it use the brand's own assets (mascot, signature product, tone)? |
| Participation | Can a whole family/group take part within 2 minutes? |
| Data capture | Does it produce consented, first-party data and trackable vouchers? |
| Cost | Hardware, production, staffing per location |
| Ease of ops | Setup time, failure modes, staff needed, throughput |
Optional: shareability, reuse after event, novelty in the local market.
Recommend the top 2–3 as one journey; the rest as add-ons tied to budget packages.

## Capacity formula for KPI slides

`capacity per day = units × (60 / minutes per turn) × operating hours`. Use 70–80% utilisation for targets; leave the final target `[ ]` until traffic data exists.
