Three things TUVA does with your organisation

AI for development organisations

TUVA's Decent AI programme helps teams use AI for real work: reports, summaries, translation, campaign ideas, analysis of community feedback. Clear rules come with it, so nobody has to guess what is allowed.

  • Role-based AI training for leaders, programme, communication and MEL staff
  • AI rules and an approval process for AI-assisted content

Project and campaign communication

Strategy, messages and multi-channel content by sector: social media, video, publications, events and communication training for local partners.

Questions we often get

How are TUVA's costs budgeted?

Costs are set per line item in your funding proposal, following the donor's cost rules. We build the budget frame with you before the deadline, and invoices follow the grant's disbursement tranches.

What is Decent AI?

Decent AI is TUVA's approach to responsible AI in development organisations: clear rules, a named person who approves, protection of participants' data, labelling of AI-assisted content, and people staying in charge of decisions.

Do you work in English?

Yes. Concept notes, budgets and logframes can be written in English or bilingually, as the donor requires.
